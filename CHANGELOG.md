# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of SuperHub, built on SuperDashboard 2.2.1 by AgP42.

- Home screen: framed clock (date, week, battery), ten pencil-sketch buttons in two rows of five, recent pages in sketch frames.
- Buttons are fully yours: name, picture (31 built-in icons or your own PNG/JPG), place, and what each opens: a folder, a note or document, an installed app, a SuperHub page, today's dated notebook, a new note or a new typed document.
- New note screen: choose name, folder, page style (Supernote styles or your MyStyle templates), handwriting recognition and orientation.
- SuperHub pages: Favourites, Starred pages, Keywords, Search, Note Clips, To-do.
- One large-type settings page; settings backed up automatically and saved copies on demand.

For SuperDashboard's own history, see https://github.com/AgP42/SuperDashboard.
